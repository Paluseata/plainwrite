Welcome to Plainwrite-K, a quiet little word processor that writes Korean too.
The 한 badge at the top (or the 한/영 key, or Shift+Space) switches between Hangul and English. The Hanja key or F9 turns the syllable before the cursor into Hanja; the choices show on the bottom line.
Plainwrite-K에 오신 것을 환영합니다. 한/영 글쇠로 한글을 켜고, 한자 글쇠나 F9로 한자를 고르세요.
